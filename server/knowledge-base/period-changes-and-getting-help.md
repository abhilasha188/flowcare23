The NHS notes that periods can change, for example by lasting longer or becoming lighter. A change does not necessarily mean there is a problem, but the NHS advises that changes should be investigated by a healthcare professional.

The NHS page says bleeding between periods, bleeding after sex, and bleeding after menopause should be checked by a doctor. It also advises contacting a GP after three consecutive missed periods when a pregnancy test is negative. These are summaries of the cited NHS page, not a diagnosis or a substitute for personalized care.

If a symptom is severe, rapidly worsening, or feels urgent, seek appropriate urgent medical care. FlowCare cannot determine the cause of bleeding or missed periods.
