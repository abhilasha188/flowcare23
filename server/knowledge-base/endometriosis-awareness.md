The NHS endometriosis page is a patient-information source about endometriosis and getting help. FlowCare cycle dates and symptom logs cannot diagnose or rule out endometriosis. Period pain or another symptom can have different causes, so a tracking app cannot determine what is causing an individual's symptoms.

For personal concerns, read the NHS information and speak with a qualified healthcare professional. Seek urgent medical care for symptoms that may require immediate attention.
