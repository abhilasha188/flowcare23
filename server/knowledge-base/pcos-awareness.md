The NHS PCOS page is a patient-information source about polycystic ovary syndrome and assessment. FlowCare cycle dates and symptom logs cannot diagnose or rule out PCOS. A chatbot must not infer that someone has PCOS from a period pattern or a list of symptoms.

For personal concerns, read the NHS information and discuss symptoms with a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use this note to make a diagnosis or change prescribed care.
