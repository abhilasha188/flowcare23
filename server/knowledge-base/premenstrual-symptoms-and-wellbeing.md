The NHS describes physical and emotional changes before a period as possible effects of changes in hormone levels. Examples on its periods page include bloating, breast tenderness, mood swings, irritability, skin changes, and headaches. People do not all experience the same symptoms, and not everyone who has periods experiences premenstrual syndrome (PMS).

The NHS says these symptoms usually improve when a period starts and may disappear a few days afterwards. A symptom pattern by itself does not establish a diagnosis or its cause. FlowCare symptom entries are personal observations, not a clinical assessment.

If symptoms are difficult to manage, affect daily life, change unexpectedly, or worry you, discuss them with a qualified healthcare professional. The NHS PMS page is available at https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/pre-menstrual-syndrome/.
