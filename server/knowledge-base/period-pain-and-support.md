The NHS period-pain guide is the primary source for current patient information about period pain. This knowledge-base note does not determine why an individual has pain and does not recommend a medicine or treatment.

Pain that is severe, changing, difficult to manage, or concerning to you is a reason to seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional. For potentially urgent symptoms, use local urgent or emergency medical services rather than relying on an app or chatbot.

FlowCare can store personal observations, but tracking cannot diagnose or rule out a condition such as endometriosis. See the NHS guide at https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/period-pain/ for its current information.
