The NHS Eat well resource provides general information and guidance about eating a healthy, balanced diet. This source does not establish a special diet that treats menstrual symptoms, and this note does not prescribe foods, supplements, or a weight-loss plan.

Nutrition needs differ between people. For advice about a personal health condition, dietary restriction, or supplement, consult a qualified healthcare professional. FlowCare observations cannot establish that a food caused or will prevent a symptom.
